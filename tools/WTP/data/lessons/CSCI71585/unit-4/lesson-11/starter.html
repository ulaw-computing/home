Learn to Code Together

Leeds Code Club is a free, friendly club for anyone who wants to learn to code, from complete beginners to experienced developers looking for a project to work on.

We meet every week in the city centre, with mentors on hand to help with whatever you are working on.

Sessions

Sessions run every Wednesday evening from 6pm to 8pm. Bring your own laptop if you have one, or borrow one of ours.

Each session starts with a short talk on a new topic, followed by time to work on your own projects, with help from our mentors.

Projects

Members have built personal websites, games, weather apps, quiz tools and much more. At the end of each term, everyone is invited to show what they have made.

If you are not sure what to build, our mentors keep a list of project ideas for every level of experience.

Join

Membership is free. Just come along to any session and introduce yourself to one of the mentors.

Under 18? Please come along with a parent or guardian on your first visit.
