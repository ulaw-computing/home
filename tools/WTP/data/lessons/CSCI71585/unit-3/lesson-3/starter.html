The Cliff Path

A dramatic coastal walk above the Atlantic. 8 km

About the Walk

This circular route follows the cliff tops for most of its length, with sweeping views of sea stacks, rocky inlets and seabirds nesting on the ledges below. It returns inland along quiet farm tracks.

The walk takes around three hours. There is a small car park and toilets at the start of the route.

Before You Go

Check the weather forecast before setting out, and wear sturdy, waterproof footwear. The cliff edges are unfenced in places.

Keep to the marked path and stay well back from the cliff edge, especially in strong winds.
