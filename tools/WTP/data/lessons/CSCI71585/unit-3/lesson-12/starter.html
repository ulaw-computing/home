Build Your First Web Page in a Day

Join us on Saturday 5 December 2026 for a friendly, hands-on introduction to building websites. No experience is needed: we start from the very beginning.

You will learn the basics of HTML and CSS, and leave with a page you have built yourself. To keep learning afterwards, the MDN Learning Area is a great next step.

The Venue

Room 2.04, The Learning Centre
12 Example Street
Leeds
LS1 1AA

Getting There

  1. Leave Leeds station by the main entrance.
  2. Turn left and walk along Boar Lane for five minutes.
  3. Turn right onto Example Street. The Learning Centre is on your left.

What to Bring

  • A packed lunch, or money for the café
  • A notebook and pen
  • Your own laptop, if you would like to keep your work
