Discover Leeds on Foot

Our friendly local guides lead small groups through the city's history, from Victorian arcades to hidden courtyards. Every tour starts and ends in the city centre.

Our Tours

Each tour takes around two hours and covers about three kilometres at a gentle pace.

Tours run every Saturday and Sunday, and on weekdays during the school holidays.

  • The Victorian City
    • Kirkgate Market
    • The Corn Exchange
    • County Arcade
  • Canals and Mills
    • Granary Wharf
    • The Leeds and Liverpool Canal
    • Armley Mills

Wear comfortable shoes, and bring a coat: our tours run in all weathers.
