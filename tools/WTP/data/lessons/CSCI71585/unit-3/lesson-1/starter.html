Study Tips for Your First Term

Starting a new course is exciting, but it can also feel overwhelming. These tips will help you build good habits from the start.

Plan Your Week

Block out time for each module in a calendar, including time for independent study. Treat these blocks like lectures: turn up, and do the work.

Practise Little and Often

Programming is a skill, and skills improve with regular practice. Thirty minutes of coding every day is far more effective than five hours the night before a deadline.

Ask for Help Early

If you are stuck, ask. Your lecturers, classmates and the library's study skills team are all there to help, and small problems are much easier to solve before they grow.
