Sea View Cottage

About the Cottage

A traditional stone cottage on the cliff top, with uninterrupted views across the bay. It has been fully renovated, while keeping its original beams and open fireplace.

The cottage sleeps four, in one double and one twin bedroom, and has a modern kitchen, a bathroom with a walk-in shower, and a sitting room with a wood burner.

The Location

The coastal path runs right past the garden gate, and the nearest beach is a ten-minute walk away. The village, with its shop, café and pub, is just under two kilometres inland.

Seabirds nest on the cliffs from spring to midsummer, and seals can often be seen in the bay below the cottage.

Facilities

  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Private parking for one car
  • Enclosed garden with seating
  • Dogs welcome (maximum two)
